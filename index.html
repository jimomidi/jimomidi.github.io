Selected Research

Recent work at Cornell & Columbia

Cross-Species Discovery of Conserved Molecular Networks in Canine and Human B-Cell Lymphoma

103 shared plasma–tumor genes; 13 conserved cancer drivers including MYC, MTOR, ARID1A, and POT1.

Mar 2026 – Now · Cornell University

Genome-Wide cfDNA Profiling of Tumor Heterogeneity in Canine B-Cell Lymphoma

Matched WGS of plasma, tumor, and germline DNA; 712 CNVs revealing distinct genomic architectures.

AgomiR-466 Therapeutic Targeting of Oncogenic Pathways in ACC

394 oncogenic transcripts prioritized as miR-466 targets, validated by docking and ADMET profiling.

ML-Optimized Blood Biomarker Panel for MDD & OCD Diagnosis

Logistic regression classifier with strong AUC/AUPRC; novel biomarkers PF4V1, FOLR3, and GZMK.
